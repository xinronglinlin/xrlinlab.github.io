Molecular Energy Materials Lab
Xinrong Lin Research Group

Molecular design of ion-conducting materials across scales.

We study how deliberately defined molecular information propagates from molecular structure to condensed-phase organization, ion transport, electrochemical interfaces and ultimately the behavior of working energy-storage devices.

Research

Our work sits at the interface of organic synthesis, polymer science and electrochemical energy storage, with a focus on connecting molecular precision to macroscopic function.

01

Molecular Design

Design and synthesis of ion-conducting molecules and polymers with precisely controlled chemical information.

02

Ion Transport

Understanding how molecular structure and condensed-phase organization govern selective ion transport.

03

Interfaces & Batteries

Translating molecular control into stable interfaces and advanced solid-state and fast-charging battery systems.
